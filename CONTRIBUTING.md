# How this org works

Kept deliberately simple.

- **Start in the [hub](https://github.com/aiassistedastronomytaskforce/hub)** (members only): your own folder,
  candidate notes, the project list, and Discussions.
- **Own projects get their own repo.** Create it from
  [`project-template`](https://github.com/aiassistedastronomytaskforce/project-template), name it however you like
  (a prefix such as `pipeline-`, `demo-`, `tool-` helps people browse), and add a row to the hub's `PROJECTS.md`.
  Owners run their repos their way.
- **Contributing to someone else's repo:** open an issue or ask first for anything big, especially large
  AI-generated changes. Forking and doing your own version is always fine; link back to the original.
- **Be honest about AI use,** and check IDs, numbers and citations against the source before committing.
- **Nothing is announced as a discovery from the org.** Use the normal channels, and credit everyone who helped.
- **Decisions** about shared things are made in hub Discussions. If it's contested, the maintainers decide.
- **Maintainers:** @troynguyen8transits (Troy Nguyen, troynguyen8@gmail.com). Inactive repos may be archived;
  that's reversible.

See also the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
